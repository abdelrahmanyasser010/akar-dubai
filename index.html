Architecture: Unified Mobile App vs Web Dashboards معمارية النظام: تطبيق موبايل موحد أم تطبيقات منفصلة؟

Why a single Unified Mobile App is technically and commercially superior. توزيع الواجهات البرمجية بين تطبيقات الموبايل ولوحات التحكم السحابية بأعلى كفاءة.

Unified Mobile App (Flutter)

Mobile-First Functions (Tenants, Landlords, Contractors) وظائف تطبيق الموبايل (المستأجر، المالك، المقاول)

  • • Direct Camera Capture: Instant photo/video upload for maintenance tickets and move-in/out inspections.
  • • Instant Push Notifications: Real-time due date alerts, approval prompts, and payment receipts.
  • • Online Checkout & Cards: 1-click mobile rental installment payments via saved cards.
  • • Owner 1-Tap Approvals: Fast mobile decision on quotes exceeding spending threshold.
  • • Technician Live Tracking: Field status updates and contractor GPS routing.
  • • Seamless Role Switching: Landlords who also rent other units switch views in 1 tap without two apps.
Dual Web Dashboards

Web Dashboard-First Functions (Managers & Accountants) وظائف لوحات التحكم (مدير العقار والمحاسب والـ Admin)

  • • Bulk Asset Entry: Adding buildings, towers, hundreds of apartments, and complex lease terms.
  • • Extended Financial Reports: Exporting detailed balance sheets, ledgers, and cashflows to PDF/Excel.
  • • Bank Reconciliation & Cheque Audit: Matching bank statements, clearing cheques, and tracking returns.
  • • Multi-Tenancy SaaS Engine: Managing real estate company plans, licensing, and RBAC permissions.
  • • Contract & Notification Templates: Customizing lease clauses, SMS, and WhatsApp message templates.

User Onboarding & Registration Architecture طرق تسجيل وانضمام المستخدمين (User Onboarding)

1. Dashboard Direct Creation

Company Admins, Accountants, and staff are created directly in the Web Dashboard by the firm owner with granular RBAC permissions.

2. Magic Link / SMS / WhatsApp Invite

When lease contracts or assets are saved, tenants and landlords receive an automated invitation link with an OTP code linking directly to their contracts.

3. SaaS Self-Signup

New real estate companies register online via website, choose their plan tier (by managed units), enter company card, and activate workspace instantly.

Certified E-Contract & Digital Signature Integration التكامل مع منصة معتمدة لإنشاء العقود والتوقيع الإلكتروني

To eliminate physical paperwork and legal friction, we propose integrating with an accredited electronic contract and digital signature gateway (such as recognized e-contracting platforms available in Egypt and the Middle East). This enables 100% remote contract generation, verified OTP/biometric signing by tenants and landlords, and complete legal enforceability with an encrypted audit trail. لإنهاء المعاملات الورقية وتوقيع العقود دون الحاجة للمقابلات الشخصية، نقترح ربط المنصة مباشرة مع إحدى المنصات المعتمدة لإنشاء وتوثيق العقود الإلكترونية والتوقيع الرقمي (مثل المنصات المتاحة والمعتمدة في مصر والشرق الأوسط)، مما يتيح إصدار العقد إلكترونياً وتوقيعه من المستأجر والمالك رقمياً بحجية قانونية كاملة وسجل تدقيق مشفر.
